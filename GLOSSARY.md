# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the language around what a learner buys, what they do with it, and what happens when they ask for their money back.

## Language

### Purchases

**Purchase**:
The record of a learner paying for a course. Each Purchase is for one course and either stands on its own (an individual purchase) or is a Team Licence. A learner can hold several Purchases of the same course over time.
_Avoid_: Order, Transaction, Sale

**Team Licence**:
A Purchase that comes with a fixed number of Seats, so a team can share one payment across several people.
_Avoid_: Bulk purchase, Group licence, Site licence

**Seat**:
One place on a Team Licence. A Seat starts unclaimed and becomes claimed when a team member redeems it to get access to the course.
_Avoid_: Slot, Spot, Invitation

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a Purchase. The team approves or declines it. One Purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request the team hasn't decided yet.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request the team has accepted. Approving it is what leads to a Refund.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request the team has turned down. It has no Refund, and the learner may raise a new Refund Request.

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount they paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund the team has approved where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the learner.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed.

**Eligibility Snapshot**:
The three values captured on a Refund Request when it is created and never changed afterwards: watched hours, days since the Purchase, and Seats claimed. They record the state the decision was made against.
_Avoid_: Audit record, frozen state

**Learner History**:
The engagement and commercial record the team sees on a Refund Request: progress, watch time, quiz attempts, rating, and comments on this course; plus this learner's Refund Requests across all courses. Informs the decision; does not gate it.
_Avoid_: Profile, Activity log, Student History
